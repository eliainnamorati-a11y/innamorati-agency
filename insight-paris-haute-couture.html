From the Front Row: Elie Saab and Georges Hobeika at Paris Haute Couture Week

Paris Haute Couture Week remains the highest expression of fashion. Every collection reflects months of craftsmanship, creative vision, and technical excellence. This season, we had the opportunity to attend the presentations of Elie Saab and Georges Hobeika, two maisons that continue to define modern couture through distinct creative identities.

Elie Saab

Elie Saab's latest couture collection embraced fantasy through a darker lens. Inspired by surrealist masquerades, the collection explored mystery, romance, and elegance with remarkable confidence.

Deep burgundy, black lace, sculptural florals, crystal embroidery, feathers, and dramatic silhouettes created a rich visual narrative. Every look carried the signature glamour that has become synonymous with the house, yet the mood felt more layered than previous seasons.

What stood out most was the restraint. The collection never relied on excess. Embellishment served the silhouette rather than overpowering it. Every detail felt intentional.

Fashion critics highlighted the collection's cinematic atmosphere and praised Saab's ability to evolve without compromising the identity that has defined the brand for decades.

From a branding perspective, this is exactly what luxury should look like. Evolution without losing recognition. Innovation without abandoning heritage. Every look remained unmistakably Elie Saab.

Georges Hobeika

Georges Hobeika presented a collection built around refinement rather than spectacle. The craftsmanship spoke before anything else.

Soft metallic fabrics, intricate embroidery, architectural draping, delicate corsetry, and fluid silhouettes created pieces that felt timeless. Every garment reflected precision. Nothing appeared unnecessary. Every embellishment had purpose.

The collection celebrated beauty through proportion, movement, and exceptional construction. Rather than chasing dramatic moments, Hobeika focused on the artistry of couture itself.

Many commentators described the collection as elegant, thoughtful, and technically outstanding. We shared that impression.

Luxury often exists in subtlety. Georges Hobeika understands that perfectly. Confidence does not require volume. The work speaks for itself.

Our Take

Watching both collections made one thing clear. Couture continues to move towards storytelling through craftsmanship.

Elie Saab delivered emotion, fantasy, and glamour with a fresh perspective.

Georges Hobeika delivered precision, elegance, and timeless sophistication.

Both maisons demonstrated the value of consistency. Their visual identities remain instantly recognisable despite each collection introducing new creative ideas. That level of clarity is what every luxury brand should aspire to achieve.

As a branding and marketing agency, we left Paris inspired by more than beautiful garments. We saw two brands that understand the importance of protecting their identity while continuing to evolve.

That balance remains one of the most valuable assets any luxury brand can possess.
