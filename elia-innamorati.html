Founder / Creative Director

Elia Innamorati

Elia Innamorati works at the intersection of investment strategy, narrative, and design, helping financial firms communicate more effectively with capital partners.

She has worked with private equity firms and asset managers in Geneva, developing a strong understanding of how institutional investors, multi-family offices, and asset managers evaluate opportunities.

Her work focuses on translating complex investment strategies into clear, structured narratives across investor materials, fundraising content, and digital platforms.
